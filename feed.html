Our FEED Services

Front-End Engineering and Design Scope

Preliminary Engineering & Specification

We provide high-fidelity engineering documentation, including detailed structural drawings and material specifications. Every design is tailored to meet load-bearing standards and technical requirements for your project.

Precision Cost Estimation & Budgeting

Eliminate financial ambiguity with Class 3 Cost Estimates, including contingencies and market fluctuation analysis. Our transparent pricing ensures accurate CAPEX planning throughout the project lifecycle.

Strategic Timeline & CPM Scheduling

Using Critical Path Method (CPM) and interactive Gantt charts, we map every task and dependency, ensuring resource optimization and a clear path to project completion.

Risk Mitigation & Contingency Planning

We proactively neutralize risks—from supply chain shifts to site constraints—before they affect your timeline or budget. This ensures smooth, predictable project execution.

Global Procurement & Lead-Time Analysis

Our team manages procurement strategy, vendor evaluation, lead-time analysis, and development of RFPs and EOIs. We secure premium materials at competitive rates while avoiding costly delays.

Statutory Approvals & Regulatory Compliance

From Environmental Impact Assessments (EIA) to building permits, we handle all regulatory approvals, ensuring your project is 100% compliant with local and international law.
