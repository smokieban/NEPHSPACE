Who We Are

Nephspace Elite Construction and Interiors Hub Ltd is a premier construction and interior design company based in Nairobi, Kenya. We specialize in delivering high quality residential, commercial, and office fit out projects to clients both locally and internationally. Our mission is to transform ideas into functional, beautiful, and sustainable spaces while ensuring the highest standards of quality and professionalism.

End to End Design and Build Solutions

Nephspace Elite Construction and Interiors Hub Ltd delivers end to end design and build solutions in Nairobi, Kenya, combining architectural innovation, world class engineering, and premium finishes.
