Procurement Support That Protects Quality, Delivery, and Budget

At NephSpace Elite Construction & Interiors Hub, we are more than just a vendor, we are your strategic procurement and supply chain partner in Nairobi, Kenya and across Africa.

From the initial design phase to the final installation, our team ensures every material, piece of equipment, and specialized service is sourced efficiently, delivered on time, and aligned with your project budget.

We combine sourcing discipline, logistics coordination, site readiness, and supplier accountability so your project keeps moving with fewer delays and stronger commercial control.

What We Deliver

Procurement Services Scope

Local & Global Material Sourcing

We manage the full lifecycle of high quality construction materials and luxury interior finishes through trusted local suppliers and global manufacturers aligned to ISO standards and design specifications.

International Logistics & Customs Clearance

We simplify international shipping, import documentation, and customs clearance to support compliant, tax efficient delivery directly to your project site.

Vetted Subcontractor Management

We identify and engage skilled specialist subcontractors through strict vetting and performance based negotiation so specialized works stay professional and within agreed scope.

Real Time Inventory & Site Logistics

Advanced tracking systems help us manage material and equipment flow so items arrive when needed, reducing waste, delays, and stock out risks on site.

Quality Assurance & Compliance

Every delivery passes through a strict inspection process to confirm compliance with technical specifications so only verified, high performance materials reach your project.
