Company Overview

NephSpace Elite Construction & Interiors Hub Limited is a premier construction and interior design company based in Nairobi, Kenya. We specialize in delivering high-quality residential, commercial, and office fit-out projects to clients both locally and internationally.

Our mission is to transform ideas into functional, beautiful, and sustainable spaces while ensuring the highest standards of quality and professionalism.

We are a multidisciplinary team of experienced professionals, including:

  • Architects
  • Engineers
  • Quantity Surveyors
  • Project Managers
  • Site Managers
  • Safety Officers
  • Technical specialists
  • Procurement Manager

Together, we bring deep industry knowledge and a shared commitment to delivering projects that are structurally sound, aesthetically refined, and fully functional.

At NephSpace Elite Construction & Interiors Hub Limited, we combine technical expertise with a client-focused approach to ensure that every project is:

  • Carefully planned
  • Efficiently executed
  • Delivered on time and within budget

We provide end-to-end solutions, covering:

  • Concept and design
  • Procurement and sourcing
  • Construction and project management
  • Interior finishing and fit-outs
  • Ongoing maintenance and support

This turnkey approach is especially valuable for diaspora and international clients, allowing seamless project management from anywhere in the world.

We work with a diverse range of clients, including:

  • Homeowners and property developers in Kenya
  • Corporate organizations and businesses
  • Kenyan diaspora investors abroad
  • International investors seeking construction partners in Africa

Our combination of local expertise and global sourcing ensures that every project meets international standards while remaining tailored to the Kenyan context.

  • Proven experience in construction, interior design, and office fit-outs
  • Strong local knowledge with global sourcing capabilities
  • Transparent communication and efficient project management
  • High-quality workmanship with attention to detail
  • Trusted partner for both local and international projects
