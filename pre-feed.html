Define the Right Project Before Major Capital Is Committed

We help you test ideas, compare options, and create a strategic foundation for industrial, commercial, residential, institutional, and specialist projects.

Our Pre FEED service focuses on project conceptualization with a practical lens. We clarify the opportunity, outline key scope boundaries, identify delivery risks, and shape the information needed for stronger investment decisions with minimal upfront expenditure.

By the end of this stage, clients have a clearer project vision, better alignment among stakeholders, and a structured roadmap for whether to proceed into FEED and detailed engineering.

What We Deliver

Pre FEED Scope

Concept Screening

We evaluate project ideas and compare strategic options against business goals, site realities, and budget direction.

Feasibility Framing

Our team develops the early technical and commercial basis needed to determine whether the project should move forward.

Scope Definition

We establish the broad functional requirements, performance targets, and initial boundaries of the project.

Risk Identification

We highlight technical, operational, cost, and delivery risks early so they can be managed before they grow.

Budget Direction

Initial cost guidance helps clients judge affordability and refine priorities before deeper design investment.

Stakeholder Alignment

We bring decision-makers onto one page by translating project intent into a shared early stage development brief.
