What We Deliver

Pre-FEED Scope

Defined Project Scope & Deliverables

We move beyond vague ideas to create a fixed Scope of Work (SoW). By identifying key milestones, operational constraints, and technical risks early, we establish a baseline that prevents "scope creep" and ensures project success from Day 1.

Process Design & Technical Concepts

Our engineering teams develop the Basis of Design (BOD), including initial process flow diagrams (PFDs), material specifications, and regulatory compliance standards. We identify technical hurdles in the cloud before they become expensive problems on-site.

Comprehensive Feasibility Studies

Is your project viable? We provide data-driven Feasibility Analysis covering economic, legal, and operational scheduling. Our actionable reports give you the "Green Light" confidence needed for final investment decisions.

Strategic Conceptual Budgeting

Replace "guesstimates" with Capital Expenditure (CAPEX) baselines. Utilizing deep industry benchmarks, we provide high-level project estimates that inform resource allocation and protect your bottom line.

Robust Project Planning (FEL-2)

We build the framework for execution. Our Work Breakdown Structures (WBS) and preliminary schedules align your engineering, procurement, and construction phases into a single, cohesive timeline.
